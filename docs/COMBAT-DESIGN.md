# Unit combat and escort design

- Zhao Yun is the main melee protector.
- Shield units absorb pressure on a lane.
- Archers attack from range.
- Healers are support placeholders and will restore ally HP in the next combat pass.
- Enemies prioritize A Dou when they are on the same lane, otherwise they pressure the nearest ally.
- A Dou wins by reaching the exit alive; A Dou at zero HP loses.
- The machine contract stays pure and deterministic. Visual particles, hit flashes, and sound remain presentation layers.
